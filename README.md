# Hi, I'm Marco 👋

I lead **Revenue Operations** for a high-growth enterprise technology company, where I turn go-to-market strategy into systems, data, and automation that scale. My work sits at the intersection of **forecasting, CRM architecture, and applied AI**, so leadership gets numbers they can trust and sales teams spend their time selling.

## 🎯 Focus Areas

- **Forecasting & Planning:** Pipeline coverage models, capacity and quota planning, and forecast-accuracy frameworks that support executive decision-making across regions.
- **AI & Workflow Automation:** LLM-powered agents and automations that remove manual work from deal desk, CRM data enrichment, approvals, and reporting.
- **Revenue Systems Architecture:** Integrating CRM, CPQ, billing, and analytics platforms into a single source of truth, with clear data governance and definitions.
- **Analytics & Insight:** Executive dashboards and self-serve reporting that connect pipeline, conversion, and bookings performance to the levers that drive them.
- **Operating Rhythm & Scale:** Designing the processes, cadences, and controls that let a revenue organization grow without adding proportional overhead.
