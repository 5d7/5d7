## 🤖 AI & Automation Approach

I treat AI as an operating capability rather than a novelty. The goal is measurable leverage on revenue outcomes.

- **Agentic workflows:** Agents that read CRM, CPQ, and call data, then reason over it to surface deal risk, draft approvals, and prepare forecast and pipeline reviews
- **Orchestration:** Event-driven automations built with **n8n**, Python, and APIs to connect systems of record without brittle manual handoffs
- **Data enrichment:** LLM-based classification, summarization, and enrichment to improve CRM data quality at scale
- **Human in the loop:** Guardrails, audit trails, and approval checkpoints so automation stays trustworthy in a regulated, finance-adjacent environment
